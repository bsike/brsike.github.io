Hi. I'm Brandon Sike.

I'm an astronomy & astrophysics PhD candidate at the University of Michigan, and a NSF graduate research fellow.

I work with Prof. Mateusz Ruszkowski on computational astrophysics. I study cosmic ray feedback in galaxies, including how cosmic rays can drive galactic outflows and affect star formation.

Projects

Tallbox Simulations with CRISP

High-resolution tallbox simulations of galaxies with cosmic ray feedback using the CRISP feedback and nonequilibrium thermochemistry model.

Cosmic Ray-Driven Galactic Outflows

Studying the impact of cosmic ray feedback on galactic outflows.

Sike et al. (2025), DOI: 10.3847/1538-4357/adda3d, arXiv:2410.06988

Star Cluster Formation

Studying the dynamical impact of cosmic rays on star cluster properties.

Sike et al. (2026), DOI: 10.3847/1538-4357/ae70ff, arXiv:2510.06134
