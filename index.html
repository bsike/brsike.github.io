Hi. I'm Brandon Sike.

I'm an astronomy & astrophysics PhD student at the University of Michigan.

I work with Dr. Mateusz Ruszkowski on computational astrophysics. I study cosmic ray feedback in galaxies, including how cosmic rays can drive galactic outflows and affect star formation.
